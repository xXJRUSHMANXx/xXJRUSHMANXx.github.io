3Deseos is a personal project where I combine my skills in **SolidWorks design**, my extensive experience in **additive manufacturing**, and my natural mindset for **problem‑solving** to create custom solutions for all kinds of needs.

Over the years, I have helped people with **functional parts, decorative pieces, personalized gifts, and practical everyday solutions**, always adapting each design to the user’s specific requirements.

---

## 🔧 Custom Functional Supports

One of the most common requests I receive is the creation of **custom supports** for objects that don’t have a commercial solution.

Some examples include:

- A tailored support for a fornt light for Xiaomi Electric Scooter.
- Holders and organizers for everyday household items such as cutlery, tools, pens, jewelry, and more.
- Adapters, clips and brackets designed to fit different items in specific environment such as a car barometer mounted on AC air vents.
- All types of supports for tubes, wiring and structural components.
-All type of enclosures for housing electronics such as Arduino boards and electronic control boxes.

Each part is designed from scratch in SolidWorks and optimized for 3D printing to ensure strength, usability and a perfect fit.

---

## 🎨 Personalized Decorative Designs

I also create **fully customized decorative pieces**, combining engineering precision with artistic creativity.

- A notable example is the creation of a 3D **moon lithosphere** with love engravings. This product wasn’t just a simple moon lamp; it could also be opened, and inside you could see a photo of the couple. The LEDs were placed around the photo holder so the light wouldn’t be annoying. I used strong magnets to make the moon parts fit together easily.

- Another example was a project for a **golf professional**, where I designed a miniature model of a golf terrain, complete with a flag and the client’s name.

- Other example of my decorative designs consists of a custom **car embellisher**, specifically personalized logos created to be placed at the center of the wheels and on various interior icons of the vehicle. This design serves not only as an aesthetic upgrade but also as a promotional element, showcasing and advertising a workshop within the automotive sector.

These pieces are unique, meaningful and tailored to the person who receives them.

## 🖨️ 3D Printing Service

Besides designing, I also offer **3D printing services** for clients who already have their own models.
I help them choose the right material, optimize the print, and ensure the final piece meets the required quality and functionality.

As a 3D printing service, I manufactured robotics components for university projects, car parts, and all kinds of designs available on the market.
---

## ♻️ Bringing Public Designs to Life

Whenever I find **useful or interesting public designs**, whether decorative or functional, I print them, improve them if needed, and adapt them to real‑world use.
This allows me to bring open‑source ideas to life and make them accessible to people who need them.

- For example, I created plant pots based on artistic pieces, such as the famous head of “David Miguel Ángel”. Essentially, I had to design a hollow interior, a drainage system, and a matching tray.
