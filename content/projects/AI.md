The world of Artificial Intelligence is opening new doors every day.
Today, we can obtain an answer to almost any question instantly.
But… **how accurate is that answer?**

My experience has shown me that AI is an extraordinary tool **only when used correctly**:
with a good prompt, a solid knowledge base, and—above all—**a final human filter**.

Every day I see how AI can hallucinate, confuse concepts or generate incorrect information.
For this reason, I believe AI should be used with intention, criteria and responsibility.

---

# Where AI Makes Sense

I use AI in many areas of my life, but always with a clear purpose.
These are the contexts where I believe AI truly shines:

### **1. Definitions and fundamental concepts**
For explaining universal, unambiguous concepts such as gravity, inertia, or Ohm’s law.
AI is extremely reliable when the information is objective and well‑established.

### **2. Languages**
AI is a **hyper‑powerful tool** for learning languages:
grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation, corrections, translations and conversation practice.

### **3. Research (with caution)**
AI can provide data that would otherwise take hours to find.
Especially useful for:
- Comparing technological products
- Understanding standards and regulations
- Exploring new engineering fields

But always with human verification.

### **4. Personal guidance**
Not as a replacement for professionals, but as a **general advisor**:
- How a tax declaration works
- How to structure a training routine
- What foods are generally healthier
- How to organise personal tasks

### **5. Financial analysis**
I am currently experimenting with **investment strategies assisted by AI**.
I propose ideas, and the AI analyses risks, diversification and logic.
I always make the final decision.

---

# How I Use AI in My Professional and Personal Projects

Below are the main areas where AI has become a fundamental tool in my daily workflow.

---

# 1) AI in Teaching

This is, without doubt, where I use AI the most today.

As a technical instructor, tools like **Gemini** and **NotebookLM** allow me to:

- Create and refine teaching programs
- Generate exercises and evaluation rubrics
- Correct texts and detect conceptual errors
- Prepare explanations adapted to different levels
- Analyse student projects more efficiently

AI has become a **pedagogical assistant**, helping me focus on what matters most:
teaching, guiding and mentoring.

---

# 2) AI in Engineering Documentation

In my personal engineering projects, AI helps me:

- Write technical reports
- Structure project documentation
- Generate specifications (plec de condicions)
- Improve clarity and coherence
- Detect missing sections or inconsistencies

It accelerates the writing process while maintaining professional quality.

---

# 3) AI in 3D Design

AI has also become a creative partner in my 3D modelling work.

For example, in the **ReFlorece** project, AI helped me:

- Generate design ideas for plant pots
- Optimise shapes for stability and aesthetics
- Explore variations and styles
- Improve ergonomics and usability

AI does not replace CAD, but it **boosts creativity** and speeds up the conceptual phase.

---

# 4) AI in Programming

I am not a programmer by background.
But with AI, I can now:

- Write scripts
- Automate tasks
- Debug code
- Understand APIs
- Build small applications

For someone without formal programming training, AI is a **game‑changer**.
It turns ideas into functional code.

---

# 5) AI in Generative Projects (Inpainting & Model Training)

One of my most advanced experiments was a **full inpainting project**, where I:

- Trained an AI model from scratch
- Used my own dataset
- Ran the training locally on my PC
- Generated images with missing regions reconstructed
- Tuned hyperparameters and model behaviour

This project taught me the fundamentals of:

- Dataset preparation
- Model training
- Loss functions
- GPU optimisation
- Generative AI workflows

It was one of the most technically challenging and rewarding experiences I’ve had with AI.

---

# Conclusion

AI is not a magic tool.
It is not perfect.
It makes mistakes, hallucinates and sometimes invents information.

But when used with **criteria, knowledge and responsibility**, it becomes:

- A teacher
- A designer
- A programmer
- A researcher
- A creative partner
- A productivity booster

For me, AI is not the future.
It is already part of my present — and a fundamental tool in my personal and professional growth.

More examples and projects will be added as my work with AI continues to evolve.
