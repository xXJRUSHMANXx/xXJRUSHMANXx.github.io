ReFlorece began with something simple: a few planters I designed and printed for my own home.
What I didn’t expect was the reaction. Everyone who saw them — friends, family, visitors — showed genuine interest. The organic shapes, the natural textures and the unusual geometry caught people’s attention immediately.

That spontaneous interest sparked the idea of turning these designs into something bigger.

---

## A Startup Born from Creativity and Nature

Together with **Marc Macià Sans**, who took responsibility for bringing the concept to the market, we created **ReFlorece**, a small startup focused on giving plants a new kind of presence through **3D printed organic design**.

My role is fully technical and creative:

- **Concept development**
- **CAD modeling**
- **Organic geometry design**
- **3D printing and production**

Marc leads the business side, ensuring the project grows in the right direction.

---

## What Makes ReFlorece Unique

ReFlorece is based on a simple but powerful idea:

> *Planters should not only hold a plant — they should complement it.*

Our designs take inspiration directly from nature.
The geometry and surface texture of each planter mimic the organic patterns of leaves, roots, bark or natural growth structures. This creates a visual harmony between the plant and its container.

### Key characteristics

- **Organic, nature-inspired geometry**
- **Unique textures that blend with the plant**
- **Fully custom designs for clients who want something different**
- **Manufactured exclusively in PLA Marble**, a material that provides:
  - a stone-like appearance
  - a premium aesthetic
  - excellent print quality
  - sustainability (biodegradable base material)

---

## Manufacturing Philosophy

All products are:

- Designed in-house
- Printed in-house
- Made with a single, consistent material
- Produced on demand to avoid waste

This allows us to maintain full control over quality, finish and design integrity.
